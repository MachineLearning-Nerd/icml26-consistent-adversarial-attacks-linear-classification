# Limitations and deviations

- Numerical scope is ℓ₂, dimension 64, and strict non-boundary cases.
- The run corroborates the finite-dimensional identity; it is not a substitute
  for the paper's proof for every ℓq geometry.
- Equality is excluded because the source mixes `>=` in the statement with `>`
  in the proof and leaves zero-margin sign behavior unspecified.
- Claims 2–5 are not exercised by this baseline node.
