# Limitations and deviations

- This route tests the theorem exactly as printed, not a silently corrected
  version that replaces `nu` by `sign(nu)`.
- The counterexample specializes to ℓ₂ and `m=0`; a single valid parameter
  setting is sufficient to falsify the quantified printed statement.
- The intended theorem likely contains a sign omission. That interpretation is
  reported separately from the verdict on the literal equations.
